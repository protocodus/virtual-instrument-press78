# PSS-780 comparison landscape — 19 October 2026

This bounded search identifies relevant alternatives, **not a measured accuracy ranking**. It found sampled PSS-family sounds and programmable engines for other Yamaha chips, but did not establish a released commercial YM3420/OPU FM engine from the first-party pages inspected. That is not proof that no such engine exists. Press78's unresolved envelope, level, DAC and exact output-circuit behavior in the [fidelity ledger](fidelity-roadmap-2026-10-18.md) remains unresolved regardless of competition.

Capabilities below are developer/producer statements observed on 2026-10-19, not independently verified product performance. No software was purchased, installed or run; no sample pack or audio/video was downloaded. Public demo links were located, not listened to or scored. Publication dates are given only where the source supplies them; otherwise the observation date applies.

## What can be compared fairly

A sampled preset can preserve details of its recorded note that a model misses. Conversely, edited loops, release envelopes, pitch interpolation and a single recorded effect state need separate evaluation; a library cannot be assumed to reproduce arbitrary operator edits. Neither architecture is inherently the winner for the user's intended task.

A defensible PSS-780 comparison would need disclosed product versions, the same known patch and notes/gates, explicit Reverb/Sustain/Chorus state, a documented capture path and fixed scoring/listening rules. Factory timbre, release behavior, editable parameters and performance response should be evaluated separately. Public musical demos and cross-family chip claims do not meet those conditions. No third-party output was rendered here, and no comparison score or market-leading claim follows.
