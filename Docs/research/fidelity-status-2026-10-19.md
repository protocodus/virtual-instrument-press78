# Press78 fidelity status, b11 and b12, 2026-10-19

Moved unchanged from the README's "Evidence and release status" and "Current
fidelity pass" sections, as of Rack prototype 0.1.0b12 and extended through b15, when the repository took
the portfolio layout. Links are adjusted for this folder. The b9 evidence is in
[native evidence, 2026-10-18](../validation/native-evidence-2026-10-18.md).

The original native run passed 42 contracts with address/undefined-behavior sanitizers. In that historical version, all 100 raw-FM presets matched the frozen Python renderer at 48 kHz, with worst absolute float-output error below `1.5e-8` after nominal delay alignment. The b11 engine intentionally differs at the corrected D1 endpoint and modulation laws; those old parity results do not validate the new behavior. The compiled analog circuit matched independent Python MNA impulses at six sample rates with maximum absolute error below `2.7e-12`. These establish implementation agreement with a model, not agreement with Yamaha hardware.

The earlier Recorded-chorus comparison is preserved in [the hardware benchmark note](native-hardware-benchmark.md) and `Tests/results/hardware-recorded-chorus.json`. On the reused recording, the three target voices improve from 5.573 to 5.110 dB mean joint stereo band error with Recorded chorus; their log-STFT score slightly worsens from 3.614 to 3.643 dB. All 100 presets improve on the joint-band metric. The observed-mid→side diagnostic improves from 1.583 to 0.404 NRMSE; this isolates the effect and does not validate the synthesized source. No new fit or independent holdout was used. The frozen baseline/candidate sources, local audition WAVs and [playable report](../../build/hardware-benchmarks/recorded-20261018T084000Z/report.html) are retained under ignored `build/hardware-benchmarks`. The earlier noise-integrated source rendered byte-identical raw, dry and Recorded full performances with noise disabled; see its historical `Tests/results/final-hardware-compatibility.json`. Earlier synthetic circuit-noise auditions under `build/noise-demos` describe that removed feature and are not current instrument demos.

The native performance report identifies its compiler, source hashes and six workloads. It excludes Rack scheduling, target translation and actual Reason CPU use. Consult the Rack adapter's build evidence for device-specific results; native tests alone do not establish host compatibility or release readiness.

The current b12 engine passes six CTest targets in both Release and ASan/UBSan, including twelve pitch contract groups. The command helper matches 49,152 converter cases, and Equal matches the b11 audio baseline byte for byte. The Rack wrapper passes 23 host-mock contracts in each mode; 9,005 static GUI checks, 437 numeric-readout cases and the five-rate latency contract pass. Intel/Apple Silicon local45 and universal45 builds, archive integrity, all 101 patches and source-aligned installed payload checks pass. Fresh protected Recon 14.0.2d7 (build 20275) CLI validation passes three b12 creations/native arm64 loads and nonlinear data conversions with exit 0. All fifteen containment probes pass; 4,900 production files, original scratch identity/content and installed payload are preserved. This establishes those CLI stages; native GUI gestures, named default-patch loading, interactive playback, song persistence and Reason CPU behavior remain unverified. Prior evidence and failed host attempts remain preserved in the Rack checkout.

The preset source is recorded in [bank provenance](../../Assets/FactoryBank/provenance.json). Public availability of a SysEx archive and the editor project's license do not establish permission to distribute Yamaha's factory bank commercially. No research videos, recordings, manual scans or executable Yamaha firmware are shipped in this repository. `Press78` has not received trademark clearance; development names and source availability are not legal clearance.

The b12 engine preserves the existing analog path and b11 envelope/modulation laws. A validated 4x component reduces numerical error against the PSS-480 continuous circuit, but worsens the reused PSS-780 joint-band score on 75/100 presets. It remains a research component and is not used by the instrument. The selected D1+LFO correction improves Musical Saw joint-band error 4.932→3.195dB and the three-target mean 5.110→4.510dB, with small regressions and worse STFT convergence recorded explicitly. All100 results and local A/B provenance are in [the b11 benchmark](hardware-fidelity-b11.md). This is descriptive reuse of the original tour, not a hardware-fidelity percentage. The new sibling recordings provide separate waveform and modulation constraints.

[The fidelity requirements ledger](fidelity-roadmap-2026-10-18.md) retains the open full-objective requirements: exact envelope/Reverb behavior, detune/scaling/velocity, OPU timing/arithmetic, the missing exact-board output circuit, independent exact-device testing and any market-leading comparison. No superlative accuracy claim follows from the current evidence.

An [optional hardware capture kit](hardware-capture-protocol-2026-10-18.md) prepares controlled envelope and Reverb/Sustain measurements using independent test voices. It generates six MIDI files without playback. Deliberate hardware playback replaces user bank 5; follow its backup and recording instructions. Its file validation is not a hardware measurement.

The follow-up envelope investigation measures a [two-stage tail in the final Sine Wave chord](exact-sine-release-2026-10-18.md). [Fixed timing](envelope-law-benchmark-2026-10-18.md) and [Reverb-cap experiments](reverb-envelope-benchmark-2026-10-18.md) improve some target/tail scores but cause large regressions elsewhere and change voice pressure. They remain research snapshots; production keeps the b11 envelope/modulation laws with the selectable b12 pitch path. [Held-note controls](envelope-capture-forensics-2026-10-18.md), [AAC/Opus comparison](codec-tail-audit-2026-10-18.md) and an [audible-event timing audit](midi-alignment-audit-2026-10-18.md) narrow the remaining capture, cutoff and alignment questions. The [OPU evidence review](opu-envelope-evidence-2026-10-18.md) separates timing, compression and unverified chip-family analogies.

The [independent timing validation](global-event-mapping-2026-10-19.md) finds patch-dependent residuals: Jazz Guitar transitions arrive earlier than the old map, while other examined patches arrive later. All three global correction candidates fail their frozen adoption criteria. A [four-map × three-model comparison](timing-sensitivity-2026-10-19.md) keeps every outcome and local A/B demo; timing changes reverse 22–26 per-patch RR×3/b11 joint-band rankings and Human Voice 1's preference. No map or release/Reverb candidate is adopted.

The [recorded mode/effect audit](recording-mode-effects-audit-2026-10-19.md) identifies Keyboard Assign `c.00` and Reverb/Chorus on in the sampled frames, with Vibrato/Sustain matching the factory flags. The manual's Mode 00 receive range excludes 18 of the published MIDI's 1,779 notes. A separate [Mode 00 benchmark](mode00-benchmark-2026-10-19.md) therefore retains 1,761 notes and all 100 program commands. Under the unchanged timing map, b11's all-preset joint-band mean changes from 3.1735 to 3.1450 dB, while log-STFT slightly worsens. Bowed Bass, Human Voice 1 and Musical Saw results are effectively unchanged. This corrects a conditional source-input fixture; it neither limits the plugin's playing range nor selects a release law or timing map. The sampled display and published MIDI are not a continuous state trace or MIDI wire log.

Further source controls constrain the next engine changes. [RKS0 slope measurements](rks0-release-slopes-2026-10-19.md) find a repeatable higher-note decay difference in the recorded composite, while the [manual/editor audit](opu-rks-evidence-2026-10-19.md) leaves the core RKS0 law unresolved. A [retrigger audit](retrigger-allocation-audit-2026-10-19.md) rejects a restricted instantaneous phase-step explanation of one deep dip without identifying voice reuse or stealing. [Selective partial losses](dac-endpoint-evidence-2026-10-19.md) rule out one common scalar gate as their sole cause; they do not recover DAC bit depth or an internal envelope cutoff. A [firsthand early-note-off account](early-noteoff-leads-2026-10-19.md) adds a separating test requirement, not an envelope-freeze rule. Artificial noise remains excluded throughout.

The [captured pitch-interval audit](pitch-grid-audit-2026-10-19.md) finds repeated compression of about 0.48 cents in the sine recording's long G/E-flat interval; b11's corresponding mid control remains within 0.0005 cents of equal temperament. A [broader pitch-pattern test](pitch-pattern-audit-2026-10-19.md) remains inconclusive under its frozen control/codec gates, rather than establishing a complete tuning table. Separately, [three author-published PSS-680 WAV packs](isolated-source-search-2026-10-19.md) provide 62 qualified stationary-note measurements across C/E-flat/G/B-flat and several octaves. They are an independent source, but may share one instrument and capture chain. A [frozen cross-source tail prediction](cross-source-pitch-prediction-2026-10-19.md) agrees closely on the upper G/E-flat contrast; its low-E-flat comparisons pass only the expanded 0.10-cent envelope and retain a systematic mismatch.

These measurements motivate a stronger [frequency-law candidate](opu-frequency-hypothesis-2026-10-19.md): one formula generates twelve rounded base values and an octave-dependent truncation, with no fitted per-note offsets. Allowing one shared tuning offset per pack, frequency RMS falls from about 0.274 to 0.003 cents across all 62 qualified samples. On the older 19 PSS-780 windows, with only two long G notes setting the common offset, RMS falls from 0.315 to 0.041 cents; the unfitted D/A-flat classes improve overall, with outliers retained. This is a posthoc candidate derived from four observed classes and evaluated on reused data, not a fresh holdout or recovered OPU arithmetic. Its copied-engine tests pass, but **b11 remains unchanged: no frequency-law selector or fitted clock offset is in the instrument, and additive noise remains absent**.

The [standalone frequency audit tool](../../Tools/audit_opu_frequency.py) reproduces the complete candidate table and scores from the tracked numerical ledger without recordings. Its optional prototype generation writes only to a separate research directory.

A later [fixed F77 prediction diagnostic](f77-frequency-prediction-2026-10-19.md) qualifies no estimator: every method fails the known-pitch finite-tail composition control. The unqualified captured estimates are all closer to equal temperament, an adverse result retained alongside the failed gates. It supplies no precise F correction and reinforces keeping the candidate outside production until isolated chromatic measurements can test the remaining classes. Its [reproduction tool](../../Tools/audit_f77_frequency.py) requires the original protocol-bound local media and preserves the failed gates.

The [sibling oscillator drawing audit](opu-sibling-clock-circuit-2026-10-19.md) verifies a nominal 5.5 MHz quartz hookup on PSS-480 and a 5.5 MHz ceramic resonator in the exact PSS-780 parts list. It supplies no FM divider, phase width or numerical aging/jitter law. The [digital-interface review](opu-digital-primary-leads-2026-10-19.md) likewise verifies a posted status-poll/write routine without identifying synthesis timing or arithmetic. The candidate's mathematical frequency word must not be presented as a recovered hardware register.
