# Target PSS-780 AM-board photographs — 2026-10-19

**New evidence:** deepsonic's original PSS-780 photo set contains complementary, 2,000-pixel-wide photographs of the AM board's component and copper sides. This is target-hardware evidence suitable for a checked board trace. It is not a recovered overall schematic, and no new circuit model or production change follows from this acquisition alone.

The earlier [exact-circuit search](exact-circuit-source-search-2026-10-19.md), [output audit](output-schematic-audit-2026-10-18.md), and [sibling-clock note](opu-sibling-clock-circuit-2026-10-19.md) were read before searching. ## What can be established visually

The component side has a boxed **AM** label. The corresponding copper-side board is marked **YAMAHA XE422**. The photographed keyboard and the AM location are explicit in the author's set. The prior Yamaha parts list calls the target AM assembly **VF108000**. Their correspondence is plausible from model, board role and component inventory; **the photograph does not expose a VF108000 stamp, and XE422 is not established as an interchangeable assembly part number**. No conclusion about all production revisions follows.

Five DIP-8 packages are visible on the AM board. The lower adjacent pair includes a readable **MN3206** marking beside the original, unmirrored `IC2` silkscreen; the neighboring package's marking is much less legible. A separate package bears a readable `4558DV` marking. The pair's placement and the prior parts list support checking the neighbor as MN3102, but its pin identity and the surrounding network must be confirmed before using a netlist. Molded orientation dots are visible; a transformed or mirrored image must not be used to read reference labels or infer pin numbers.

Several silkscreen values are clear enough to constrain a future trace: **68P, 390K and 2.7K** near the paired DIP packages; **220P, 1000P, 47K and 22K** elsewhere in the adjacent analog section. In conventional board-value notation these are candidate 68 pF, 390 kΩ, 2.7 kΩ, 220 pF, 1,000 pF, 47 kΩ and 22 kΩ values. Their visibility establishes marked values and placement, **not which IC pins they connect to**. Ambiguous markings resembling `6800` or `3900` are deliberately not converted to numerical component values: a final glyph may be a unit symbol, and the component type/bands must be checked.

The copper photo exposes substantial continuous routing and solder joints. Tape, the speaker leads, foam, the heatsink and perspective obscure some locations. No continuity measurement, board removal by this project, supply measurement, oscillator waveform or component tolerance has been obtained. In particular, proximity of 68P/390K to a clock-driver candidate does not establish the timing circuit or its effective resistance. It does not yield a justified BBD clock, delay excursion, gain, distortion, or noise law.

## Bounded search outcomes and next step

The new useful result is the target photo pair. A second first-hand lead, [Pete Hollobon's PSS-680 repair page](https://hollobon.com/techblog/yamaha_pss680_fix.html), was found through [Kevin's original editor article](https://diyelectromusic.com/2023/08/02/arduino-pss-680-synth-editor-part-1/), but ordinary requests returned a gateway/timeout error and a TLS-handshake failure; no repair-page images were acquired. The [Altervista service catalogue](https://www.manuals.altervista.org/) lists PSS-680/780 manuals but the inspected entries were not public document links and the site proposes contact-based exchange. No contact was made. Additional bounded English, Japanese and Russian repair/board queries did not deliver another readable target drawing. These are outcomes of the inspected links and queries, not proof that no complete manual or photograph exists.

The next defensible step is an independently checked mirrored-side registration and a small pin-to-pin net ledger around MN3206/MN3102, with every passive assigned to visible pads and unresolved crossings left open. An initial approximate mounting-hole homography was unsuccessful and is retained as `trial-warp.png`/`trial-homography.npy`; it must not be treated as an accepted alignment. Its mirroring also makes the original `IC2` resemble `IC5`. A complete filter or clock model should wait for that trace and a separate review, rather than inherit PSS-480/PSS-560/PSS-270 values by similarity.

The [machine-readable ledger](target-am-board-2026-10-19.json) binds the acquired photographs, source pages, inspection artifacts, prior ledgers and bounded failures. **Source/DSP, native wrapper, GUI, presets and version files were not edited; no artificial noise or new hardware-fidelity claim was introduced.**
