# Press78 fidelity requirements and current evidence

The active objective is a premium, synth-focused PSS-780 model, validated against public circuit documentation and real recordings. A claim that it is the market's most accurate model remains unproven. This ledger preserves that full objective while recording concrete progress; neither passing software contracts nor a better score on one reused video completes it.

## Current evidence-backed work

- **Operator waveforms:** a newly acquired PSS-680 carrier-only table experiment supports the current four waveform identities, including signed squared sine. It does not identify numerical ROM precision or DAC encoding. See [recording audit](recording-holdout-audit-2026-10-18.md).
- **D1 envelope endpoint:** the zero-height D1 stage no longer takes an unwanted decrement before D2. Controlled sibling behavior and explicit model endpoint tests support this bounded correction; factory changes are small. See [FM audit](fm-fidelity-audit-2026-10-18.md).
- **Modulation:** reused PSS-780 pitch contours support about 5.24 Hz triangle modulation, with approximately 12.5 cents at PMS3 and 100 at PMS6. The frozen candidate improves pitch tracking on separately acquired PSS-580 Saw and Human Voice passages, with patch-identity and per-passage phase qualifications. Unobserved sensitivity levels and exact reset/delay rules remain hypotheses. See the [pitch audit](lfo-pitch-audit-2026-10-18.md), [preregistered candidate](lfo-candidate-protocol-2026-10-18.json) and [independent recording evaluation](recording-holdout-audit-2026-10-18.md).
- **Analog integration:** the documented PSS-480 surrogate has a validated4x research component. End-to-end interpolation/circuit/decimation reduces its48kHz10kHz numerical magnitude error from1.43dB to0.076dB against the same continuous network. A tested80-host-frame matched-delay integration was rejected as the instrument default after it worsened75/100 PSS-780 comparisons. The shipping path retains its prior32-tick latency. Numerical accuracy to the sibling circuit does not establish exact-device agreement. See [schematic audit](output-schematic-audit-2026-10-18.md) and `Tests/results/analog-oversampled.json`.
- **No added noise:** white and circuit-noise generation remain absent. Legacy noise properties are hidden/inert. Fidelity research does not override the user's explicit removal requirement.
- **Two-stage release evidence:** the exact-device tour's final pure-carrier chord has a fast release followed by a much slower tonal tail near 18–20 dB below its held level. Fixed-frequency/window analysis is independently reproduced. A fixed Reverb-cap experiment improves this extended tail, but global timing/cap candidates retain broad regressions and unresolved endpoint/allocation behavior. The b11 envelope laws remain in the shipping engine. See the [measured tail](exact-sine-release-2026-10-18.md), [fixed timing experiments](envelope-law-benchmark-2026-10-18.md) and [Reverb experiment](reverb-envelope-benchmark-2026-10-18.md).
- **Capture separation:** a simultaneously held sine weakens a common-gain-only explanation of the released sine's two stages. Final partials disappear at different times in both AAC and Opus, excluding an Opus-transcode-specific or final-PCM16-only cause. Shared upstream processing remains unresolved; no new cutoff is adopted. See the [forensic controls](envelope-capture-forensics-2026-10-18.md) and [codec comparison](codec-tail-audit-2026-10-18.md).
- **Benchmark timing:** the first 18 selected instant-attack audible transitions are 22–63 ms later than the frozen MIDI mapping, but separate metadata-selected Jazz Guitar transitions are 55.7–68.1 ms earlier. The [global mapping validation](global-event-mapping-2026-10-19.md) rejects all three fixed corrections; neither the old map nor a replacement is established as actual delivered MIDI timing. The [original audit](midi-alignment-audit-2026-10-18.md) and its failures remain preserved.
- **Timing and model selection:** a fixed [four-map × three-model experiment](timing-sensitivity-2026-10-19.md) reproduces all three prior unchanged-map renders byte-for-byte and retains all 100 scores for every scenario. Human Voice 1 favors the slower release/Reverb candidates under the old map and b11 under every corrected scenario. There are 22–26 per-patch RR×3/b11 band-ranking reversals. The full 100 mean band ordering remains b11, RR×3, then RR×3+Reverb, while STFT-convergence ordering reverses. No map or envelope replacement is selected by these reused-data scores.
- **Pitch and repeated-note behavior:** [fixed cross-pitch fits](rks0-release-slopes-2026-10-19.md) find steeper decay for MIDI 87 than 79 at raw RKS0 in mid/pair measurements; moving stereo channels, prior-note history and harmonic overlap qualify the inference. The [manufacturer/editor evidence](opu-rks-evidence-2026-10-19.md) establishes encoding but not the complete rate law. A [retrigger dip](retrigger-allocation-audit-2026-10-19.md) exceeds what a restricted instantaneous phase-only step predicts, without identifying a physical allocator or delivered off/on gap.
- **Endpoint separation:** [fixed before/after windows](dac-endpoint-evidence-2026-10-19.md) show MIDI 87 dropping 35–38 dB while other partials fall only 2–3 dB in AAC and Opus. One common scalar gate cannot be the sole explanation. Per-voice retirement, postmix quantization and shared upstream processing remain candidates; no DAC word length or hardware threshold is inferred.
- **Static pitch intervals:** the [captured pitch audit](pitch-grid-audit-2026-10-19.md) retains repeated compression of about 0.48 cents in the long MIDI 67/75 interval, while the corresponding b11 mid control remains within 0.0005 cents. The [broader pattern test](pitch-pattern-audit-2026-10-19.md) cannot generalize that result under its frozen control/codec gates. Its failed estimates and narrower conditional contrasts remain constraints on the frequency candidate below, rather than evidence for a fitted per-note table.
- **Digital interface:** a [primary disassembly review](opu-digital-primary-leads-2026-10-19.md) independently verifies the posted poll/write software pattern; its mapping to the FM block remains provisional. Conditional standard-65C02 cycle counts do not establish the Yamaha CPU clock, FM cadence, register meanings or arithmetic precision. No artificial scheduling quirk is added from those counts.
- **Additional source leads:** a [firsthand early-note-off account](early-noteoff-leads-2026-10-19.md) motivates equal-known-RR tests. Ordinary asymmetric release already provides a competing explanation, so no special freeze rule is added. The [separate PSS-680 hardware/editor tutorial](soden-pss680-source-audit-2026-10-19.md) supplies readable settings and transport constraints, but no defensible new numerical law: its linked editor uses single-voice SysEx dumps, Program Changes and a one-second auto-put timer.

The latest [mode/effect audit](recording-mode-effects-audit-2026-10-19.md) identifies `c.00` and Reverb/Chorus on in sampled frames; Vibrato/Sustain match the factory flags. Applying the manual's Mode 00 reception range to the published MIDI excludes exactly 18 notes, leaving 1,761 notes and all 100 inferred program commands in a [separate benchmark fixture](mode00-benchmark-2026-10-19.md). It modestly reduces aggregate band errors but slightly worsens log-STFT; all three focus voices and the earlier model orderings remain effectively unchanged. The fixture does not change b11's playing range or establish continuous hardware state, actual MIDI arrival times or an envelope law. Historical unfiltered results remain intact.

An [independent source inventory](isolated-source-search-2026-10-19.md) adds 62 qualified stationary-note interiors from three author-published PSS-680 packs. The files may share one unit/capture chain, and their musical labels and patch associations are inferred. Their relative pitches predict the reused PSS-780 tour's upper G/E-flat contrast closely; [the separately frozen cross-source test](cross-source-pitch-prediction-2026-10-19.md) accepts low-E-flat comparisons only within its expanded 0.10-cent tolerance, retaining the bass-related mismatch. These are captured-frequency constraints, not an independent exact-device holdout or a chip-arithmetic measurement.

The resulting [frequency-law hypothesis](opu-frequency-hypothesis-2026-10-19.md) uses one rounded twelve-note formula and octave truncation. With one shared tuning offset per pack, its all-62 frequency RMS is about 0.003 cents versus 0.274 for equal temperament; on the original 19 PSS-780 windows, calibrating only two long G notes gives 0.041 versus 0.315 cents. Unfitted D/A-flat classes improve overall, while the D outlier and conflicting conditional intervals remain recorded. The law was derived posthoc from four observed classes, and the copied-engine validation does not turn reused measurements into a fresh holdout. At that stage it remained a proposed behavioral candidate, without a fitted unit-clock offset or additive noise; the subsequent b12 update below distinguishes the recovered command table from this initial formula.

A subsequent [F77 tail diagnostic](f77-frequency-prediction-2026-10-19.md) retains both metadata-selected events, the original G calibration and all failed gates. Analytical controls pass, but every estimator fails the known-pitch finite-tail render control, leaving neither event evaluable. All unqualified capture estimates favor equal temperament over the candidate. That adverse descriptive direction remains a constraint; the test neither establishes a precise F correction nor supports adopting the complete law.

The [oscillator circuit evidence](opu-sibling-clock-circuit-2026-10-19.md) independently establishes a nominal 5.5 MHz quartz hookup for PSS-480 and a 5.5 MHz ceramic component in the PSS-780 parts list. It does not establish the candidate's factor, rounding order, phase width, synthesis divider or jitter/aging values. Those physical distinctions remain open even when a mathematical frequency law closely matches the recordings.

## Market scope

The [updated primary-source comparison inventory](comparison-landscape-2026-10-19.md) separates sampled PSS-family products, hardware editors, patch translations and programmable engines. [Plogue's own PortaFM page](https://www.plogue.com/products/chipsynth-portafm.html) identifies OPLL/YM2413, OPL2/YM3812 and OPK2/YM7129. The current [discoDSP OPL page](https://www.discodsp.com/en/opl/) identifies a YMF262/OPL3 engine, superseding the earlier partial-OPL3 description. Neither page establishes an exact YM3420/PSS-780 model. No third-party audio was rendered or scored, and this bounded search is not proof that no other competitor exists or support for a superlative accuracy claim.

Completion requires measured agreement in the still-open behaviors above, plus complete instrument validation. Each further change should preserve raw source/capture provenance, separate calibration from evaluation, retain regressions and explain what is still unidentified.

The [optional controlled capture protocol](hardware-capture-protocol-2026-10-18.md) prepares the next envelope investigation: independently authored pure-carrier voices, fixed gates, TL/rate sweeps and four matched Reverb/Sustain conditions. Its six MIDI files and 60 cases pass file-format, checksum and schedule validation. Generation performs no playback; deliberate use writes user bank 5 and requires the documented backup. No hardware measurements have yet been made with this kit.

## Lo-fi preservation and recovered command paths

The no-noise requirement excludes added hiss while preserving authentic signal-dependent color. DAC transfer and quantization, level-dependent distortion, BBD behavior, component tolerances, supply filtering and clock behavior remain fidelity requirements. Supply-dependent gain/headroom and clock effects must remain distinct from additive hum, hiss, dither and clock leakage, which stay excluded by the noise-removal requirement; the current creative Drive and safety limiter must not be relabelled as measured hardware distortion. The [new signal-path audit](lofi-signal-path-audit-2026-10-19.md) traces separate regulated and unregulated sibling rails, local OPU/mixer filtering, and the exact manual's overload condition. It also identifies the low-voltage opamp characterization gap and the missing target AUX tap. No generic random voltage, bit-depth or aging setting follows from those sources.

The [local supply model](local-supply-model-2026-10-19.md) now implements the sibling's documented47ohm/470uF AVDD41 branch as separate voltage/current ports with exact constant-input time integration. Independent AC and RK4 circuit checks pass. It remains research-only: actual load and voltage-to-audio/clock sensitivities are unidentified, so it adds no production modulation or noise. The default instrument's sound is unchanged.

An [original firmware release audit](opu-release-command-path-2026-10-19.md) now separates Sustain's SRR selection plus immediate keyoff from Reverb's operator control bit. Independent original-program execution reproduces the commands, including early-off cancellation and live rate refresh. At that checkpoint the conventional pedal and absent chip-Reverb mode remained implementation gaps; their internal timing was still unknown. This rules out treating a blanket RR multiplier as a complete reconstruction.

The [key-level command audit](opu-level-command-path-2026-10-19.md) recovers paired-note signed tables, endpoint plateaus and integer saturation. The separately validated native prototype improves all100 band means by about 0.053–0.058 dB and slightly improves STFT log error, but Bowed Bass regresses 0.179–0.209 dB on all four maps, failing its fixed 0.1 dB limit. The [complete experiment](hardware-key-level-b13-2026-10-19.md) therefore rejects default adoption. Its source patch, successful software checks and all adverse scores remain available for the next amplitude/FM-transfer investigation. Production source and existing circuit, clock, drive, chorus and noise settings remain unchanged.

The separately frozen [posthoc component diagnostic](key-level-decomposition-2026-10-19.md) gives Bowed Bass unchanged-map differences of +0.156114 dB for the carrier change alone and +0.055765 dB for the modulator change alone. This narrows the current renderer's regression, including relative note/chord balance; it does not establish the physical attenuation or DAC law. Human Voice 1's nonadditive errors rule out treating these scores as universal additive contributions. No hybrid is adopted. Original-program execution of the [physical panel mapping](front-panel-level-map-2026-10-19.md) establishes the interpretation needed for a future overload comparison: the manual's literal display range over 90 corresponds to raw carrier TL 0–8, and Melody Volume 9 contributes no further attenuation. No nonlinear threshold or supply response is fitted from that warning.

## B13 release-pedal implementation and conditional FM constraint

The [Sustain policy update](sustain-policy-2026-10-19.md) implements the verified rate-selection/keyoff distinction. Another 224 original-program cases and 64 independent cases establish factory/program flag seeding followed by CC override, including old cached patch rates after program changes. Original now selects RR/SRR live and releases immediately; legacy behavior remains the missing-property default, with new Init/PSS files explicitly opting into Original. Mode changes wait for voices and steal fades to finish. Future-only patch snapshots and short steal fades remain explicit plugin adaptations, not a full keyboard program/allocator model. Reason's normalized pedal threshold is retained separately from the raw MIDI API's nonzero rule.

All seven core targets pass normally and with sanitizers, with 17 new Sustain groups and 11 independent public-API groups. Legacy comparisons preserve 39,321,600 audio bytes across 100 patches, three rates and both pitch laws. All eight complete no-CC64 performance renders match b12, preserving the four-map recording scores exactly. The wrapper separately passes 34 contracts and frozen-b12 audio comparisons, including the retained witness for a same-frame pedal-edge compatibility defect. These checks establish the implemented policy, not a measured replacement envelope law. The authored toggle versus runtime override and unavailable host program identity remain beta limits; Reverb, physical envelope timing, DAC and power behavior remain open.

The separately frozen [conditional FM/feedback experiment](fm-level-conditional-2026-10-19.md) rejects the earlier assumption that the published Saw recipe had zero feedback: the author's recipe and original panel mapping both identify maximum Feedback 07. Across eight stationary low notes, the current joint primitive has calibration/validation harmonic errors of about 0.479/0.554 dB. A two-parameter fit reaches 0.168/0.310 dB but fails its required held-out improvement and per-note conditions; numerical qualification also fails, with both interpretations of control scope preserved. No FM scale or TL slope changes follow. This narrows the case for a large FM-depth correction while leaving source association, capture response and separate level/phase scaling unidentified.

An [original envelope-command audit](opu-envelope-command-audit-2026-10-19.md) now verifies the complete Sine99-to-SynthBass1 recipe on both firmware images: raw D2R24/22 and RKS0 reach the chip unchanged across12slots and notes36–96. All27,758 declared cases pass, with earlier fixture failures retained. This rules out a CPU pitch-rate macro in those tested paths, conditional on the captured sound actually following the authored recipe; it does not identify chip-internal keycode/rate arithmetic. Physical Reverb history also survives selecting99 and the recipe edits, leaving the recording's effects state unknown. No envelope law changes follow from these command results alone.

Two follow-up measurement experiments retain negative results. The [phase-aware PM separation](pm-envelope-separation-2026-10-19.md) fails its frozen synthetic/native controls before any new Bass inference; independent diagnostics identify dynamic-window/weak-line error and additional profile uncertainty. The [EditingFun tonal-line test](editingfun-tonal-2026-10-19.md) keeps every original support but rejects all six source fits. Its nearby-tone control also demonstrates false acceptance, so even a clean-looking tonal slope would not prove physical envelope isolation. The new tools narrow the analysis gap without selecting a physical envelope law or changing the instrument.
