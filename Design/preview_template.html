The front holds 35 main sound controls. The rear holds 30 advanced options: key scaling, sustain release, AM and coarse detune, modulation setup, model selection and hardware calibration. Cyan and lavender knobs distinguish the two operator envelopes.

This design preview edits interface state and does not play audio. Envelope controls use rate codes, not seconds. Level increases clockwise; OFF is fully counterclockwise. Higher envelope rates are faster, and D1 attenuation lowers the first decay target. Reason’s standard patch-name and patch-browse widgets are represented by the selector above; all 100 factory patches use the PSS prefix. Sockets are placeholders.

The native front uses the photorealistic faceplate. Controls and lettering remain live; the rear and folded panels use the original material. Init and factory presets start with the Real output profile; individual controls remain editable.
